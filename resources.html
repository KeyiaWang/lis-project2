New to these terms? Start here.

What is implicit bias?

Implicit Bias is an unconscious and unintentional belief or thought about a specific group of people that changes and influences the way that a person beahves towards that specific group. An example of this is something like resume screening, where two idnetical resumes recieve much different treatment, because one has a traditional white name, and another has a traditional muslim name.

The Implicit Association Test (IAT)

I thought that the IAT test from Harvard was very interesting, and unlike anything else I have ever done before. Before taking the test, I was curious to see how something could test implicit bias, and I think that the IAT test does as good a job as possible to test for something that is unconscious. However, I think it is impossible to truly understand implicit bias inside of a person, as it is something that is unconscious, and not on purpose.

Intersectionality

Intersectionality is the idea that people's race, gender, and other such factors that define their identity, impact and shape their expieriences in the world. Further, it is the idea that a single person's expierience cannot be understood by looking at only one part of their identity, you must look at all parts.
